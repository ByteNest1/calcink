CalcInk

Write an arithmetic expression ending in =. The answer appears beside it and updates live as you edit.

  • Vocabulary — digits 0–9, + − × ÷ (or a slanted /, or a raised dot ·), decimal point, =
  • Check mode — write your own answer after = and get a ✓ or ✗
  • Scratch-out — scribble back and forth over ink to delete it
  • Insight — the eye button shows what the recogniser sees, with per-symbol confidence
  • Private — recognition runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Works offline after the first visit.
